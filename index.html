It started with one machine

A good cup can
bring people together.

Fernhill opened with a second-hand espresso machine, six tables and a simple idea: coffee tastes better when nobody is in a hurry.

Today we work with three small roasters, bake daily and host a weekend market for local makers. The machine is still here. We just gave it a bigger friend.

Fair sourcing

We pay growers above fair-trade price and publish where each bag comes from.

Less waste

Compostable cups, a refill discount and day-old bakes shared with a local shelter.

Community first

Open mic on Thursdays, book swap on Sundays and a table always free for students.
